Will it run on my Mac?+

The current build requires macOS 14 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer). Model choice and available memory affect transcription speed.

What do I need to set up?+

Grant Microphone access to record your voice and Accessibility access to use the shortcut and paste into other apps. Choose and download a local model; System Check helps you verify your setup.

Is it free?+

Linty is free and open source under the MIT license. Local transcription does not require a subscription. Optional Groq services use your own API key and are subject to your provider’s limits and pricing.

What if a transcription isn’t right?+

Review and edit it in History, add unfamiliar names to your dictionary, or try another speech model. Audio quality, language, and background noise can affect results.
